What to expect

How We Work: No Surprises, Start to Finish

We know from experience that what homeowners want most is simple: someone who turns up when they say they will, keeps the disruption to a minimum, and leaves the place clean. That's the standard we work to on every job.

  • 1
    Site Visit & Quote

    We visit the property, assess what needs doing, and give you a clear written quote with no obligation. No vague estimates — a real number you can plan around.

  • 2
    Preparation & Protection

    Before a brush touches a wall, we protect floors, furniture, and surfaces properly. Good preparation is what separates a lasting finish from one that fails in 12 months.

  • 3
    Painting & Finishing

    Professional application using quality materials appropriate for the surface, the room, and — for exteriors — Cornwall's coastal climate specifically.

  • 4
    Clean Handover

    We leave the space clean, tidy, and exactly as agreed — nothing left to chance. If there's anything you'd like checked before we go, just say.
