We take on plastering work across Cornwall, so surfaces are properly prepared before painting.

What we offer

Typical plastering work includes:

  • Patch repairs, including cracks and damaged areas
  • Making good after building works
  • Making good after electricians and plumbers
  • Skimming walls and ceilings ready for paint
  • Plastering ready for decoration

If a room needs more than a straightforward repair or skim, we will tell you up front and quote accordingly — no surprises mid-job.

10% off decorating after full plastering

Have whole walls or full rooms plastered by us and we’ll take 10% off decorating them afterwards. The discount applies to full-wall and full-room plastering, not patch repairs or small areas of making good, and it will be shown clearly on your quote.
