Recruitment

We work with reliable painters and decorators who maintain high standards, communicate clearly, and turn up consistently.

What we look for

  • Strong preparation and finishing standards
  • Reliable attendance and professional conduct
  • Comfortable working in occupied homes or live site environments
  • Clear communication and a practical, team-first approach

What you can expect

  • Clear work scopes and site expectations
  • Consistent standards and straightforward coordination
  • Professional communication and fair treatment
  • Ongoing opportunities where performance is strong

Requirements

  • Right to work in the UK
  • Public liability insurance (or willingness to obtain)
  • UTR and CIS registration (or willingness to register)
  • Own tools and PPE suitable for the work
  • References or examples of completed work where available
