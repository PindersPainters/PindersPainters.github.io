Set room prices: prep, paint and woodwork sorted

We paint homes across Cornwall. These are set prices for a full room refresh, covering the ceiling, walls, all woodwork and all the prep that goes with it. Pick your room from the list, so you're not waiting on a vague “from” quote for a standard bedroom or lounge.

How to measure: multiply the floor's length by its width. If you're between two sizes, pick the larger one or send us a photo and we'll confirm. Open-plan spaces, vaulted ceilings and rooms outside these sizes still get a quote, just not a set price.

What's included

  • Ceiling, prepared and painted
  • Walls, prepared and painted
  • All woodwork in the room: doors, frames, skirting, architraves and window boards
  • Normal preparation: sanding, filling, caulking, undercoat where needed, dust control and a tidy finish
  • White ceiling paint, undercoat, filler and caulk

Paint

Paint is included in the set price. We supply it, so it's one number, job done.

Want a colour our usual brand doesn't do, or a specific product? We'll supply it at cost from our supplier, Rabbarts, shown clearly on your quote. Labour and prep stay at the set price.

Not included in the set price

  • Heavily damaged plaster or major making good beyond normal filling
  • Full wallpaper stripping
  • Damp or mould treatment beyond spot prep
  • Spray finishes, specialist coatings or multi-colour feature schemes
  • Kitchen units and cupboard spraying
  • Tiling or bathroom suites
  • Exterior work
  • Access equipment beyond household stepladders

If a room needs more than a standard refresh, we'll tell you up front and quote the extra. No surprises mid-job.
