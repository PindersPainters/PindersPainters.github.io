The difference

Why We're Cheaper Than a Typical Agency

Labour agencies add a margin on top of every painter's rate — every day, for the entire duration of your contract. That margin compounds fast on a large programme. Pinder's removes it entirely.

You deal directly with a painting contractor who supplies their own people. There's no third party taking a cut between you and the person with the brush.
