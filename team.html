About Pinder's Painters · Cornwall

Meet the Team

Pinder’s Painters is a Cornwall painting and decorating business built around straight talking, reliable attendance, and a finish that lasts — for homeowners and for contractors who want painters without agency markup. Here’s who’s behind the work.

Ben Pinder

Founder & Owner

Ben founded Pinder’s Painters to give Cornwall clients direct access to professional painters — no agency in the middle. Based around Truro, Newquay and Perranporth, the team covers residential interiors and exteriors plus contractor labour supply across the county. Expect clear quotes, tidy sites, and a finish checked before we leave.
