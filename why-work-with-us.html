Our approach

What Makes Us Different

We're not a recruitment agency and we're not a one-person band. We operate as a painting contractor — which means consistency, accountability, and no middlemen.

01

Relationships, Not Recruitment

We work with a consistent group of experienced painters — not a rotating pool of agency workers. That means you get the same standards, the same faces, and genuine continuity across projects.

02

Straightforward Pricing

Clear day rates or project prices with no hidden fees, no agency-style margins, and no surprises on the invoice. You know exactly what you're paying for before a brush touches a wall.

03

Consistent Workforce

Our painters know how to work together and within live environments. That consistency supports productivity, quality of finish, and reliable site attendance — even when the programme shifts.

04

Long-Term Reliability

We're not interested in filling one gap and moving on. Our aim is to be the painter supplier or decorator that clients call back project after project — because the experience was straightforward and the work was good.
